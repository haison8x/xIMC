RAFFLES MATHEMATICAL OLYMPIAD (RMO) ROUND 1 - PROBLEM CATEGORISATION
============================================================
Total problems: 340, 2008-2025 (no paper for 2020).
The contest was called RIPMWC (RI Primary Mathematics World Contest) before 2024.
IDs: Year-Problem. The text was typed by hand from the scanned papers.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. NUMBER THEORY  [64 problems]
     1.1  Divisibility, remainders, last digits & cycles  (32)
     1.2  Divisors, primes, HCF / LCM & integer equations  (32)

     2.1  Sums of sequences: telescoping & geometric  (23)
     2.2  Substitution, grouping & common factors  (29)
3. DIGITS & PATTERNS  [40 problems]
     3.1  Digits: digit sums, digit counts & number structure  (20)
     3.2  Number arrays, sequences & repeated processes  (20)
4. WORD PROBLEMS  [53 problems]
     4.1  Ratios, percentages, mixtures & equations  (23)
     4.2  Motion, clocks & work rates  (30)
5. GEOMETRY  [64 problems]
     5.1  Areas & area ratios of polygons  (24)
     5.2  Circles, arcs & shaded regions  (17)
     5.3  Angles, lengths & solids  (23)

     6.2  Counting figures, paths & recursion  (15)
     6.3  Logic, pigeonhole, games & extremal problems  (24)
